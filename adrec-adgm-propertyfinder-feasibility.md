# ADREC/DARI and ADGM Property Finder Data-Source Feasibility

**Research date:** 30 Aug 2026

## ADREC/DARI and Madhmoun

ADREC confirms that Madhmoun is the governmental MLS for Abu Dhabi and describes API-based publication for real-estate professionals. DARI’s public **Corporate Integration** service page, however, is marked **Coming Soon** and does not publish an API specification, access method, or listing-data endpoint. It offers a business contact path for companies that wish to integrate with ADREC systems.

This supports a compliant future route: the company should request an authorized Madhmoun corporate integration or broker feed. The public pages do not establish an immediately callable API that can return Property Finder’s external listing ID, listing URL, date published, advertised price, or full advertisement status.

## ADGM and AccessRP

ADGM real-estate services are administered through AccessRP and govern property services in ADGM’s jurisdiction, specifically Al Maryah and Al Reem Islands. Its advertising-permit service, launched with DMT/ADREC and Madhmoun, requires permits for advertisements in that jurisdiction and is intended to improve verified advertising. The public material describes regulation, permits, service access, and real-time service data; it does not publish a Property Finder listing feed or API.

Accordingly, ADGM is not a source for Saadiyat listing monitoring. It may provide a separate compliance route only for Al Maryah and Al Reem advertisements, subject to AccessRP authorization.

## Implication for the requested weekly monitor

Neither public ADREC/DARI nor ADGM content presently provides the full Property Finder advertisement fields required for the weekly monitor. A genuine Property Finder feed/API or a company-authorized Madhmoun broker/corporate integration must supply the external advertisement data. Municipal permit and registry data can support verification, but cannot substitute for a portal listing’s external ID, URL, publish date, price, or current online status.
