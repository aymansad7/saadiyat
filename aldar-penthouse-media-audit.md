The current World of Aldar unit explorer and `unit-detail` responses for the requested Baccarat, Nobu, and Mandarin Oriental penthouses contain no unit-specific image/media fields. The Arthouse public launch page publishes a project gallery but does not identify a penthouse unit.

Mandarin Oriental's official Aldar launch page separately publishes two assets explicitly named as penthouse gallery images. They are displayed only on the two documented 5-bedroom Mandarin Oriental penthouse cards and are labelled as **official category-level project-gallery visuals**, not images of either exact unit.

The local preview browser was unauthenticated during visual capture, so it stopped at the secure-session gate. TypeScript and the mapping unit test validate the conditional card display; an authenticated Master Admin should verify presentation after deployment.
