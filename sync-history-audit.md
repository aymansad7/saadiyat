# Sync History Detail Audit

**Reviewed:** 4 September 2026

The Sync History interface now labels every price event with the explicit prior and new values. A missing prior price is shown as **“First price published: Not published → AED …”** rather than the ambiguous label “Price Changed.” Operational status and official World of Aldar source-state transitions are likewise displayed as **previous → new** values, while the latter remains separate from NAS availability.

For **Al Ghadeer Parks** in the rolling 36-hour window ending on 4 September 2026, the audit recorded 453 baseline unit records, followed by 453 first-price publications from the exact matched workbook: 280 in Parks 1 (NC) and 173 in Parks 2 (ND). There were no actual prior-price-to-new-price revisions, no operational status changes, and no official source-state changes in that window. Every current Parks unit is recorded with World of Aldar source state **New**; this is not treated as NAS resale availability.

The associated Excel report is generated by `scripts/export_alghadeer_parks_changes_report.ts`. It contains a summary, a row-level change log, and the current Parks state. The report contains no owner/contact data.

Unit-email provenance is served only through the Master-only `propertyOwners.unitContacts` procedure. It collates exact reviewed owner links, current unit-card contact, and append-only historical email changes with role, source label, and first/last observed date. Non-Master callers are rejected by the existing Master-only access gate.
